and, for mac, its published README. The pinned upstream manifests were also
checked. Source URLs and hashes are recorded in index.json.

No copyright statement was found in these published packages or their pinned
upstream trees. Author fields are attribution metadata, not invented copyright
claims. The accompanying canonical license texts state the declared terms; they
must not be mistaken for recovered package-specific upstream notices. Any
copyright placeholders in canonical texts remain unfilled templates.
