These adaptations are distributed under LibRaw's CDDL-1.0 option, and are also

in build-info/libraw.json. The original vendor source and pin are unchanged.

The adapters parallelize independently indexed Nikon HE stripes, repair their
coefficient carry rows before reconstruction, and implement LibRaw's virtual
processing hotspots with the same numerical operations. Development settings,
transfer curve, sensor geometry and final color/orientation policy are retained.
Adapter source is included in this distribution under build-info/native/.
